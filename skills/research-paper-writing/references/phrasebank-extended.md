## 1. Defining terms（定义术语）

**用途注释**：学术写作中定义术语有两种目的——向导师/读者证明你理解该词，以及让读者明确"某些关键词在此处的确切所指"。当关键词含义不清时，误读便会产生。常用策略：①术语溯源（谁最早使用）；②"三分定义"骨架（X is a Y which/that …）；③坦承定义分歧并给出本文采用的定义。

**术语溯源与引入**
- The term 'X' was first used by Smith (1985) to refer to …
- The term 'X' can be traced back to the Greek word for …
- The term 'X' was introduced by Smith in her 1998 study of …
- Historically, the term 'X' has been used to describe …
- In 1987, psychologist John Smith popularized the term 'X' to describe …
- The terms 'X' and 'Y' were first used by Smith (1954).

**给出定义（三分骨架）**
- It is necessary here to clarify exactly what is meant by 'X'.
- X is an institution where knowledge is produced and passed on to others.
- X may be defined as the branch of economics [which is] concerned with the measurement, causes, and consequences of social problems.
- X can broadly be defined as any stimulus that is capable of eliciting a response.
- X can be defined as … It encompasses …
- X is a broad/umbrella term that refers to … covering a wide range of …
- In the literature, the term 'X' tends to be used to refer to …
- Whereas X refers to the operations of …, Y refers to the …
- X is defined by Smith (2015: 119) as '…'.
- A similar definition has been proposed by Smith et al. (2015), who have argued that X means …

**指出定义分歧 / 难以定义**
- There are multiple definitions of X, and there is little consensus about what X actually means.
- The definition of X has evolved over time / has been broadened in recent years.
- 'X' is a contested term. / X is challenging to define because …
- A precise definition of X has proved elusive. / A generally accepted definition of X is lacking.
- Despite its common usage, X is used in different disciplines to mean different things.

**界定本文用法**
- In the present study, X is defined as … / The term 'X' is used here to refer to …
- In this dissertation, the terms 'X' and 'Y' are used interchangeably to mean …
- While a variety of definitions of 'X' have been suggested, this paper will use the definition first suggested by Smith (1968), who saw it as …
- The term 'X' is not used here in the conventional sense.

## 2. Classifying and listing（分类与列举）

**用途注释**：分类 = 依据共同点把对象分组并命名，借此理解同类事物的共性、辨认类间差异；列举 = 系统呈现一组信息，其顺序本身可暗示重要性排序。两者都常用于文章开头引入新话题，或用于文献综述梳理流派。

**对主题进行分类**
- X can be classified into Xi and Xii.
- X can be categorised into Xi, Xii and Xiii.
- X may be divided into three main categories: a) …, b) …, c) …
- X may be classified according to / on the basis of / depending on Y into Xi and Xii.
- It has become commonplace to distinguish 'Xi' from 'Xii' forms of X.
- X is generally classified into two types: Xi, also known as …, and Xii or …
- There are two basic approaches currently being adopted in research into X. One is …
- X can be grouped into a number of different types according to the degree of …
- Various types of X can be grouped under one heading.

**特定分类体系**
- Smith (2015) draws a distinction between …
- Smith (1987) distinguishes between systems that are a) …, b) …, or c) …
- Smith's (1980) typology of X is the one most widely used.
- In Smith's system, individuals are classified as belonging to upper or lower categories of …
- For Smith, X is of four kinds: (1) X which …; (2) X which …; (3) X which …; and (4) X which …

**列举信息**
- Xs have three basic functions. Firstly, they … Secondly, they … Thirdly, they …
- This topic can best be treated under three headings: X, Y, and Z.
- The key aspects of X can be listed as follows: X, Y, and Z.
- The disadvantages of the new approach can be discussed under three headings, which are: …
- There are three reasons why X has become so dominant. These are: …

**转述他人分类**
- Smith and Jones (2020) list X, Y and Z as the major causes of failure.
- Smith (2020) suggests three conditions for X. Firstly, X should be …; secondly, it needs to be …; and thirdly, it must …
- Smith (2011) classified Y into three distinct types using …

**评价分类体系**
- This system of classification is useful because it helps distinguish …
- This system of classification has withstood the test of time / provides a basis for …
- This system of classification is somewhat arbitrary / is now out of date / is in need of revision.

## 3. Describing quantities, trends, and proportions（数量、趋势与比例）

**用途注释**：描述数据包含两个维度——**方向与幅度**（上升/下降/持平、缓和/急剧、峰与谷）和**量的表达**（分数、百分数、均值、范围、比例）。非母语写作者最易出错的，是介词与代词（over half of / nearly a third of / a 65% increase in）的搭配。

**趋势与预测（升、降、持平、峰谷）**
- The graph shows that there has been a slight/steep/sharp/steady/gradual/marked rise in the number of …
- What is striking in this table is the rapid decrease in the incidence of X.
- Production of X peaked in 1985, before falling sharply the following year.
- The number of Xs reached a peak during the late 1990s.
- Production of X reached a low point in 2015. / The rate fell to a low point of $5.00 at the end of the year.
- Growth remained steady at around 4 per cent, after which the figure levelled off.
- The rate of X is likely to / will probably / is projected to / is expected to fall by 12 per cent after 2035.
- an initial rise followed by a steady decrease

**分数与百分比**
- Over half of those surveyed indicated that …
- Nearly half of the respondents (48%) agreed that …
- Almost two-thirds of the participants (64%) said that …
- Less than a third of those who responded (32%) indicated that …
- Of the 148 participants who completed the questionnaire, just over half indicated that …
- Since 1981, England has experienced an 89% increase in crime.
- The response rate was 60% at six months and 56% at 12 months.

**均值、范围与比例**
- The mean age of Xs with coronary atherosclerosis was 48.3 ± 6.3 years.
- The respondents had practised X for an average of 15 years (range 6 to 35 years).
- Rates of decline ranged from 2.71 to 0.08 cm per day (Table 11), with a mean of 0.97 cm per day.
- X had the lowest proportion of Y at only 14 per cent.
- The proportion of the population attending university in 1990 was 65% higher than in 1980.
- Cold weather may kill up to ten times as many people as hot weather.

## 4. Describing cause and effect（因果）

**用途注释**：因果表达分四类语法载体——**动词**（cause / lead to / result in）、**名词**（the cause of / a consequence of）、**介词短语**（owing to / as a result of）、**连接词/副词**（Therefore / thus / thereby）。更重要的是**区分"已确立的因果"与"仅仅可能的因果/关联"**：后者必须弱化（may contribute to / appears to be linked to），否则会过度断言。

**动词表因果**
- Lack of iron in the diet may cause / can lead to / can result in / can give rise to tiredness and fatigue.
- Scurvy is a disease caused by / resulting from / stemming from a lack of vitamin C.
- Much of the instability in X is driven by / stems from / can be attributed to the economic effects of the war.

**名词表因果**
- One reason why Xs have declined is that …
- The most likely causes of X are poor diet and lack of exercise.
- A consequence of vitamin A deficiency is blindness.
- X can have profound health consequences for older people.

**介词短语与连接词**
- Around 200,000 people per year suffer from X owing to / because of / as a result of / as a consequence of poor diet.
- If undernourished children do survive to become adults, they have decreased learning ability. Therefore, / Consequently, / As a result (of this), when they grow up, it will probably be difficult for them to find work.
- Malnutrition leads to illness and a reduced ability to work in adulthood, thus / thereby perpetuating the poverty cycle.
- The warm air rises above the surface of the sea, creating an area of low pressure.

**影响因素（contributory agency）**
- X is a significant contributory factor to the development of …
- X has contributed to the decline in …
- A number of factors play a role in determining the effects of …
- Several factors are known to affect / influence / determine X.
- X is generally seen as a factor strongly related to Y.

**仅可能的因果 / 关联（弱化表达）**
- X may be an important factor in … / X may play a vital role in bringing about …
- X may have been caused by an increase in …
- There is some evidence that X may affect Y.
- X appears to be linked to Y. / This suggests a weak link may exist between X and Y.
- It is not yet clear whether X is made worse by Y.

## 5. Comparing and contrasting（比较与对照）

**用途注释**：通过比较异同深化双方理解，比较也常是评价的前奏（比出哪个更有用）。两种组织方式：**句内比较**（用 while / whereas、介词短语 In contrast to、比较级 more…than、对比动词 differs from）与**跨句比较**（用 By contrast / Similarly / Likewise 起第二句）。

**引出差异**
- X differs from Y in terms of … / in a number of important ways.
- There are a number of important differences between X and Y.
- Smith (2003) found significant differences between X and Y.
- In contrast to earlier findings, however, no evidence of X was detected.
- Women and men differ not only in physical attributes but also in the way in which they …

**引出相似**
- X is comparable to Y in terms of …
- Both X and Y share a number of key features.
- The effects of X on human health are similar to those of Y.
- These results are similar to those reported by Smith et al. (1999).
- The approach used in this investigation is similar to that used by other researchers.

**句内对照（三件工具）**
- Oral societies tend to be more concerned with the present, whereas literate societies have a very definite awareness of the past.（while / whereas）
- In contrast to people in oral cultures, people in literate cultures organise their lives around clocks and calendars.（介词短语）
- Smith's interpretation differs from that of Jones (2004), who argues that …（对比动词）
- Women are more/less likely than men to perform well in tests of …（比较级）
- Women tend to have greater/less verbal fluency than men.

**跨句对照与类比**
- By contrast, / In contrast, / On the other hand, many people in oral societies do not know the calendar year of their birth.
- Similarly, / Likewise, / In the same way, low level adult learners need graded input supplied in most cases by a teacher.
- The results were compared with those obtained in previous studies.
- The difference is only marginal; in most respects the results were relatively uniform.
- The same is true of … / The same applies to …

## 6. Giving examples（举例）

**用途注释**：例子既可作为**证据**支撑概括性论断，也可用来**解释陌生概念**（教学场景尤多），还便于记忆。技术上分两种位置：例子作**句子主信息**（An example of X is …; For example, …）与作**附加信息**（such as …; including …）。此外可"以个案为证据"（This case demonstrates …）。

**例子作主信息**
- A classic example of X is …
- For example, Smith (2015) conducted a series of semi-structured interviews in …
- People begin smoking for a variety of reasons. They may, for example, be influenced by …
- Another example of what is meant by X is …
- This is exemplified in the work undertaken by …
- An example of this is the study carried out by Smith (2004), in which …
- This is certainly true in the case of …
- The most dramatic illustration of this is …
- The case of X illustrates the nature of …

**例子作附加信息**
- People begin smoking for a variety of reasons, such as pressure from peers or …
- Many diseases can result at least in part from stress, including arthritis, asthma and migraine.
- The prices of resources, such as copper, iron ore, and aluminium, have declined over …
- Pavlov found that if some other stimulus, for example the ringing of a bell, preceded the food, the …

**以个案作为证据**
- This case demonstrates the need for better strategies for …
- Overall, these cases support the view that …
- As this case very clearly demonstrates, it is important that …
- This case study confirms the importance of …
- It is evident from the examples given here that …
- The evidence presented thus far supports the idea that …
- Take, for example, the case of … / As a case in point, consider …
- The following quote illustrates …

## 7. Being cautious / Being critical（谨慎表达与批判性表达）

**用途注释**：二者是学术语气的两根支柱。**谨慎（hedging）**——在存在少量不确定性时避免绝对断言、在存在少数例外时避免过度概括，并可与他人命题保持距离；讨论部分尤多。**批判**——不是"反对"，而是审视来源、指出论证或方法的缺陷，并走向**建设性改进**（constructive criticism）。分列如下。

### 7A. Being cautious（谨慎）
- It is thought that … / It is believed that … / It has been reported that …
- According to Smith (2002), … / Many scholars hold the view that …
- This inconsistency may be due to … / A possible explanation for this might be that …
- The observed increase in X could be attributed to …
- These findings cannot be extrapolated to all … / These data must be interpreted with caution because …
- We cannot exclude the possibility that … / The possible interference of X cannot be ruled out.
- Taken together, these results suggest that … / The evidence from this study suggests that …
- In general, the study found a tendency for …
- X tends to attack cells and breaks down tissues.
- Ozone is toxic to most / almost all / some types of living organisms.
- Ozone levels often / generally / frequently exceed WHO levels in many cities.
- This phenomenon may / could / is likely to become more common in the future.

### 7B. Being critical（批判）
- Much of the criticism that X has attracted relates to …
- These claims have been strongly contested in recent years by a number of writers.
- Previous studies of X have not dealt with … / have only focused on …
- Most studies in the field of X have only focused on …, rather than on Y.
- The study is limited in that it relies solely on … / it does not take into account …
- The main weakness with this theory is that … / The key problem with this explanation is that …
- A major problem with this experiment was that no control for X was used.
- Smith seems to ignore … / Smith fails to grasp that … / Smith's interpretation overlooks …
- However, such explanations tend to overlook the fact that …
- This method of analysis has a number of limitations.
- Approaches of this kind carry with them various well-known limitations.
- The study would have been more interesting if it had included …（建设性建议）
- These studies would have been more useful if they had focused on …（建设性建议）
- A more comprehensive study would include all the groups of …

## 8. Writing about the past / Referring to the present（时间性表述）

**用途注释**：英语时态在学术写作中的分工——**一般过去时**用于过去某一具体时点的单一事件（常配 In 1933 / From 1933 to 1945 等时间状语）；**现在完成时**用于过去与现在相连、或近期多方参与的研究（常配 To date / So far / Over the past decade）；**一般现在时**用于当前状态、普遍认识与现行理论。

**一般过去时＋时间状语**
- In 1933, / Three years later, / From 1933 to 1945, restrictions were placed on German academics.
- For centuries, / Throughout the 19th century, authorities in X placed restrictions on academics.
- The link between X and Y was established in 2000 by Smith et al.
- Prior to the work of Smith (1983), the role of X was largely unknown.
- It was not until the late 1960s that historians considered X worthy of scholarly attention.
- The first systematic study of X was reported by Smith et al. in 1992.
- In 1960, Smith et al. introduced a system of classification based on …

**现在完成时（过去与现在相连 / 近期研究）**
- To date, little evidence has been found associating X with Y.
- Up to now, the research has tended to focus on X rather than on Y.
- It is only since the work of Smith (2001) that the study of X has gained momentum.
- So far, three factors have been identified as being potentially important: X, Y, and Z.
- Over the past few decades, the world has seen the stunning transformation of X, Y and Z.
- Several recent studies have revealed that …
- X has been intensively investigated recently due to its …
- Recently, these questions have been addressed by researchers in many fields.

**一般现在时（当前状态与普遍认识）**
- The situation today is that …
- At present, we are unable to … / Until recently, very little was known about …
- To date, very few examples of X have been found.
- X is now obsolete / is a relatively recent phenomenon.
- Current theory, as it stands, does not adequately account for …

## 9. Signalling transition and ordering ideas（过渡与组织衔接）

**用途注释**：过渡句像给读者的"路线图"（road map）——必须准确且易于跟随。功能包括：**预告**（本节/下文将讨论什么）、**重提**（回到前文话题）、**转向**（进入新话题）、**小结本节**、**预告下一节**，以及**增补/转折/对比**的连接词。

**预告结构**
- The following is a brief description of …
- In the section that follows, it will be argued that …
- In this paper, I argue that … / The central thesis of this paper is that …
- This paper has been divided into four parts. The first … / This first section provides a brief overview of … It then goes on to …
- The following part of this paper moves on to describe in greater detail the …

**转向与重提**
- Turning now to the experimental evidence on …
- Having defined what is meant by X, I will now move on to discuss …
- So far this paper has focused on X. The following section will discuss …
- With regard to X, … / As far as X is concerned, … / In terms of X, …
- As discussed above, … / As explained in the introduction, it is clear that …
- As was pointed out in the introduction to this paper, …

**增补、对比与反对**
- In addition, it is important to ask … / Another significant aspect of X is …
- In contrast to Smith, Jones (2014) maintains … / Unlike Smith, Jones (2014) has argued …
- However, this system also has a number of serious drawbacks.
- Despite this, little progress has been made in the …

**小结本节与预告下节**
- To conclude this section, the literature identifies …
- This section has reviewed the three key aspects of …
- In summary, it has been shown from this review that …
- The next chapter describes synthesis and evaluation of …
- In the next section, I will present the principal findings of the current investigation …
- A summary of the main findings, together with …, is provided in the next chapter.

## 10. Describing figures, tables, and data（图表与数据描述）

**用途注释**：图表描述有三块内容——**指称**（用什么句式把读者带到图表：table X shows / as seen in figure X）、**趋势**（rise / decline / peak / remain constant / an initial rise followed by …）、**版面与数据来源**（top left of the figure、shaded area、data were compiled from …）。规则提示：正文指称图表应使用编号而非"above / below"，图例中出现的缩写须在图表下方定义。

**指称图表**
- Table X shows … / Figure X illustrates …
- The graph shows that there has been a marked fall in the number of …
- As seen in figure X, …
- Examples of X are shown in figure X.
- X is summarised in figure X / X is given in the appendix.
- The image shows that …

**读图：趋势与位置**
- … peaked at … / a peak of … / … remained constant / shows an upward trend.
- an initial rise followed by a steady decrease
- The shaded area represents … / Values in brackets are … / All values are given as …
- marked by a dotted line / shown by a solid line
- the top left of the figure / in the centre of the graph / the bottom right of the figure

**数据来源与说明**
- Data on X were collected … / We obtained data on X from …
- The data were compiled from a number of sources.
- a random sample of … / X was selected at random from …
- The bulk of the data was collected …
- No data were available for X.
- Supplementary data can be obtained from the authors.

## 11. Writing an abstract（摘要专用句）

**用途注释**：研究论文/学位论文摘要应让读者快速纵览整项研究。典型要素为：**话题重要性**＋**文献背景**＋**研究空白** → **研究目的**（必需）→ **方法** → **关键发现**（必需）→ **意义与价值**。其中"研究目的"与"关键发现"是两项**强制性**要素。注意：除"发现的意义"外，各要素句式亦可出现在正文引言中。

**话题重要性 / 文献背景**
- X plays a key role in … / X is fundamental to … / X is a major contributor to …
- The importance of X is indisputable.
- Several studies have documented …
- A growing body of evidence suggests …
- Recent studies related to X have shown that …
- X has been the focus of much investigation in the search for …

**指出研究空白**
- However, X has yet to be understood.
- The mechanisms underlying those defects are still poorly understood.
- Previous studies of X have not dealt with …
- For the past three decades, studies of X have been restricted to …
- No known empirical research has focused on exploring relationships between …

**研究目的**
- This study set out to examine …
- The aim of this study was to …
- The principal objective of this project was to investigate …
- The present study aimed to explore the relationship between …

**方法**
- The research is based on four case studies.
- This study provides a novel approach to quantifying X using …
- An online survey provided quantitative data from 670 participants.
- A cross-sectional study was undertaken to explore the potential relationship between …
- A combined qualitative and quantitative methodological approach was used to …

**关键发现**
- Results showed that … / The findings show that …
- The principal findings of this research are that …
- The findings indicated that there was a positive relationship between …
- Analysis of X revealed that … / The experimental data suggested that …
- Significant associations for X were identified for ten variables, including …

**意义与价值**
- The results of this study support the view that …
- An implication of this is the possibility that …
- The findings can contribute to a better understanding of …
- This research provides a timely and necessary study of …
- This study should, therefore, be of value to practitioners wishing to …

## 12. Writing a cover letter to the editor（投稿信）

> **来源提示**：*Academic Phrasebank 2023* 与 *PhraseBook* 均**未设此专章**，以下按国际期刊投稿信的通行格式与惯用语整理。功能顺序通常为：**称呼 → 说明投稿与稿件类型 → 简述背景与创新/价值 → 原创性与伦理声明 → 推荐/回避审稿人 → 致谢结尾**。

**称呼与投稿陈述**
- Dear Editor-in-Chief, / Dear Dr X, / Dear Professor X,
- We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled 'X' for consideration for publication in [Journal Name].
- Enclosed please find our manuscript 'X', which we would like to have considered for publication as an original research article in [Journal].
- We wish to submit the enclosed manuscript, 'X', for exclusive consideration by [Journal Name].

**简述与价值**
- This study investigates …, and we believe it will be of great interest to the readership of [Journal].
- To the best of our knowledge, this is the first study to …
- We believe our findings will contribute to a better understanding of … and inform practice in …
- This work is a natural fit with the scope of [Journal], because …

**原创性与伦理声明**
- The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere.
- All authors have read and approved the final version of the manuscript and agree to its submission to [Journal].
- All authors have made a substantial contribution to the work and have no conflicts of interest to declare.
- The study was conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki and approved by the Ethics Committee of X (approval no. X).
- Written informed consent was obtained from all participants.

**推荐 / 回避审稿人与结尾**
- We suggest the following potential reviewers, who are experts in the field: Dr A, Dr B and Dr C.
- We would like to request that Dr X be excluded as a reviewer, owing to a potential conflict of interest.
- We thank you for your consideration and look forward to hearing from you.
- Thank you for your time and consideration; we look forward to your response.

## 13. Responding to reviewers / Writing a response letter（审稿回复信）

> **来源提示**：同第 12 类，两本 PDF 均无此专章，以下按国际期刊"逐条回复"（Comment / Response）通行规范整理。体裁要求：**先致谢、后回应**；每条评论单独列出并逐条应答；语气礼貌但就事论事；写明修改位置（页/行）。分**同意修改、部分同意并解释、礼貌拒绝**三类措辞。

### 13A. 通用开场与致谢
- We thank the editor and the reviewers for their careful reading of our manuscript and for their constructive comments.
- We are grateful to the reviewers for their helpful suggestions, which have substantially improved the manuscript.
- We have carefully considered all the comments and have revised the manuscript accordingly. All changes are highlighted in the revised text.

### 13B. 同意修改（agree）
- We agree with the reviewer and have revised the manuscript accordingly (see lines X–Y on page Z).
- As suggested, we have added a new section on … (p. X, lines Y–Z).
- We thank the reviewer for pointing this out; we have now corrected this error throughout the text.
- Following the reviewer's suggestion, we have reanalysed the data using … and reported the results in Table X.
- We concur with the reviewer; accordingly, we have clarified the definition of X in the Methods section.

### 13C. 部分同意并解释（partially agree, with explanation）
- We agree that X is an important issue; however, we respectfully note that our data do not allow us to …
- We thank the reviewer for this comment. While we agree in principle, we have chosen to retain … because …
- We acknowledge the reviewer's concern, but we believe that our approach is justified because … We have, nevertheless, added a sentence acknowledging this limitation.
- The reviewer is right that …; however, we would argue that … We have now revised the text to make this clearer.
- We have partially addressed this by …; a full investigation of X is, however, beyond the scope of the present study and would be an avenue for future work.

### 13D. 礼貌拒绝（politely decline）
- We respectfully disagree with the reviewer on this point, for the following reason(s): …
- We thank the reviewer for this suggestion; however, we feel that it falls outside the scope of the present study.
- We agree that this would be interesting, but we believe it is more appropriately addressed in a separate study, as it would require …
- We have considered this suggestion carefully; unfortunately, we are unable to implement it because …
- We would politely decline to … as this would …; we hope the reviewer will agree that the current version nevertheless addresses the underlying concern.

## 14. Describing limitations（局限性表述，比 hedging 更细）

**用途注释**：局限性与前述"谨慎表达"的区别在于——hedging 是**逐句**弱化断言，而局限性通常**成段**自我审视，并需按层面分类：**设计/样本/测量/范围/数据/理论/统计**。写作要诀有两点：①说明局限"为何"限制结论（不能只罗列）；②用"局限 → 未来研究"的衔接收尾，把缺陷转为贡献空间。

**研究设计与方法层面**
- This study is limited in that it relies solely on self-report data.
- The research is limited by its reliance on a single case and the absence of a control group.
- The research is limited by the fact that it is cross-sectional in design and was restricted to …
- This experiment used poorly matched controls, which may have introduced …
- The study suffers from multiple design flaws, including …
- A major weakness with this study is that there was no control for X.

**样本与推广性**
- The main limitation of this study is that only 10 participants were included.
- The sample size was relatively small, and caution must be applied, as the findings might not be generalisable to …
- These findings cannot be extrapolated to other populations because …
- The study suffers from selection bias, which limits the external validity of the findings.
- Selection bias is another potential concern because …

**测量与数据**
- These data must be interpreted with caution because …
- The lack of a standardised measure makes it difficult to interpret these results with confidence.
- We cannot exclude the possibility that the results have been confounded by …
- A further limitation is the retrospective nature of the data, which were collected over 30 years ago.
- These findings may be somewhat limited by the lack of information on …
- The possible interference of X cannot be ruled out.

**变量、因果与解释范围**
- The study does not take into account variables such as …, and this may have influenced …
- The observed associations do not establish causality; X may be a consequence rather than a cause of Y.
- It remains unclear whether X also applies to …
- The precise mechanism for X remains unclear and is beyond the scope of this study.

**收尾：由局限转向未来研究**
- Despite these limitations, the present findings provide a useful basis for future research.
- As with all such studies, there are limitations that offer opportunities for further research.
- The limitations identified here point to several promising avenues for future investigation.
- Further work is required in order to determine whether …

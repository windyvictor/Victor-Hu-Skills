## 一、按写作任务分类的实用句式库

### 1. 介绍研究背景与领域重要性（Establishing the territory）

**领域内重要性：**
- X is a central concept in … / X is at the heart of our understanding of …
- X has long been a question of great interest in a wide range of fields.
- X has been the subject of much systematic investigation.
- Studies on X represent a growing field.
- One of the most significant current discussions in X is …

**时间框架型（近年的兴起）：**
- Recent years have witnessed a growing academic interest in …
- In the last few decades, there has been a surge of interest in the effects of …
- The past thirty years have seen increasingly rapid advances in the field of …
- Recently, there has been growing/renewed interest in … / a growing number of publications focusing on …

**社会/现实重要性：**
- X plays a key/vital/crucial role in …（注意：plays a(n) + adj. + role in 是高频骨架）
- X is fundamental to … / The importance of X is indisputable.
- There is evidence that X plays a crucial role in regulating …

**作为"待解决的问题"引入：**
- X is a major problem in … / One of the greatest challenges …
- Despite its safety and efficacy, X suffers from several major drawbacks:
- There is an urgent need to address the safety problems caused by …

### 2. 指出研究空白（Identifying a niche / gap）

**无人研究：**
- No previous study has investigated X.
- To date, no large-scale studies have been performed to investigate …
- There is a surprising lack/paucity of studies investigating …
- Up to now, far too little attention has been paid to …

**虽有研究但不充分/有缺陷：**
- Previous studies of X have failed to explore/consider/take account of …
- However, much of the research up to now has been descriptive in nature.
- Most studies of X have only been carried out in a small number of areas.
- Previous studies have suffered from small sample sizes / inadequate research design.
- The generalisability of much published research on this issue is problematic.

**已知中未知（推荐句型——承认已知+点出未知）：**
- It is now well established that … However, the influence of X on Y has remained unclear.
- What remains unclear/unknown, however, is why … / is whether …
- However, (exactly) how X affects Y remains unclear / is still not fully understood.
- The mechanisms that underpin X are not fully understood.
- Much less is known about X. / Research to date has not yet determined …

**证据矛盾型：**
- Evidence for X has been mixed. / To date, studies investigating X have produced equivocal results.
- There is conflicting evidence on the relationship between X and Y.

### 3. 陈述研究目的与贡献（Occupying the niche）

**目的（实证研究常用过去时）：**
- This study set out to investigate/assess/determine …
- The aim/purpose of this study was to …
- The specific objective of this study was to …
- The current study aimed to address these gaps in the existing literature by …

**目的（论文/学位论文常用现在时）：**
- This paper/thesis aims to/seeks to …
- The central thesis of this paper is that …
- Specifically, the following issues will be addressed: …
- This research seeks to address the following questions: …

**贡献与意义：**
- This is the first study to …
- This study sheds new light on … / fills a gap in the literature by …
- Therefore, this study makes a major contribution to research on X by demonstrating …
- The findings should make an important contribution to the field of …
- Understanding the link between X and Y will help …

**界定范围与结构：**
- It is beyond the scope of this study to examine …
- This paper has been divided into four parts. The first part deals with …
- The remaining part of the paper proceeds as follows: …

### 4. 描述方法（Describing Methods）

**指明本研究方法：**
- A mixed-methods approach is employed in this research.
- This study uses/utilises/employs a qualitative case study approach to investigate …
- This investigation takes the form of a case-study …

**说明选择理由：**
- X was selected for its reliability and validity.
- A case study approach was used to allow a deeper insight into …
- One advantage of the X analysis is that it avoids the problem of …

**描述过程（过去时+被动语态是标配）：**
- Data were collected using semi-structured interviews.
- Participants were recruited from 15 clinics across X.
- The experiments were conducted using custom software written in …
- Statistical analysis was performed using SPSS software (version 26).
- X was prepared according to the procedure used by Smith et al. (1990).

**目的引导：**
- To rule out the possibility that …, the participants were …
- In order to control for bias, measurements were carried out by another person.

**方法局限：**
- The small size of the dataset meant that it was not possible to …
- The responses relating to X were subjective and were therefore susceptible to recall bias.

### 5. 报告结果（Reporting Results）

**指向图表：**
- Table 1 shows/presents/compares an overview of …
- As shown in Figure 1, … / It can be seen from the data in Table 1 that …

**突出显著数据：**
- What stands out in this table is …
- The most striking result to emerge from the data is that …

**正面结果：**
- A significant difference was found between X1 and X2, t(11) = 2.906, p<0.01.
- There was a significant positive correlation between …
- A two-way ANOVA revealed that …

**负面结果（null results）：**
- No significant differences were found between …
- No evidence was found for non-linear associations between X and Y.
- X appeared to be unaffected by Y.

**意外/有趣结果：**
- Interestingly, a significant interaction of X and Y was also observed.
- Surprisingly, only a minority of respondents …
- This result is somewhat counterintuitive.

**质性问题/访谈：**
- Five broad themes emerged from the analysis.
- One issue/theme that emerged from the interviews was …
- As one interviewee put it: '…' / Over half of those surveyed reported that …

### 6. 讨论与解释结果（Discussing Findings）

**与文献一致（支持性比较）：**
- This finding is consistent with that of Smith (2000) who …
- These results are in line with those of previous studies.
- This study supports evidence from clinical observations (e.g. Smith, 2007) that …
- These results corroborate the findings of a great deal of the previous work in …

**与文献矛盾：**
- This outcome is contrary to that of Smith et al. (2020) who found …
- However, the findings of the current study do not support the previous research.
- In contrast to earlier findings, however, no evidence of X was detected.

**解释结果（必须带 hedging）：**
- A possible explanation for this might be that …
- This discrepancy could be attributed to …
- These differences can be explained in part by …
- There are several possible explanations for this result. Firstly, …

**承认局限：**
- With a small sample size, caution must be applied, as the findings might not be …
- These data must be interpreted with caution because …
- We cannot exclude the possibility that …
- The possible interference of X cannot be ruled out.

**提出假设与含义：**
- These findings suggest that … / The present study raises the possibility that …
- It can thus be suggested that … / It is possible, therefore, that …

### 7. 结论与意义、未来工作（Writing Conclusions）

**回扣目的（现在完成时）：**
- This study set out to … / This paper has argued that …
- The aim of the present research was to examine …

**总结发现：**
- This study has shown that …
- The most obvious finding to emerge from this study is that …
- Taken together, these results suggest that …

**陈述贡献：**
- The findings reported here shed new light on …
- The present study has extended our understanding of …
- This is the first study which has examined the associations between …

**局限+让步（limitation 与贡献并置）：**
- Notwithstanding these limitations, the study suggests that …
- Despite its exploratory nature, this study offers some insight into …
- A major limitation of this study is the … / The generalisability of these findings is limited …

**未来工作：**
- Further research is needed/required to establish whether …
- Future studies should focus on … / include …
- More research on this topic needs to be undertaken before …
- Another possible area of future research would be to investigate why …

**实践/政策建议：**
- The findings of this study have a number of important implications for future practice.
- A reasonable approach to tackle this issue could be to …

### 8. 综述文献：支持性引用 vs 批评性引用

**文献总体概述：**
- A large and growing body of literature has investigated …
- Much of the current literature on X pays particular attention to …

**支持性引用（综合同一阵营）：**
- This view is supported by Jones (2015) who writes that …
- Similarly/Likewise, Jones (2016) makes the case for …
- In the same vein, Smith (1995) in his book XYZ notes …
- There is a consensus among social scientists that … (e.g. Smith, 2019; Jones, 2020)
- Smith's seminal study provides a valuable insight into …

**批评性引用（指出弱点）：**
- However, Smith's analysis does not take account of X, nor does he examine …
- The existing accounts fail to resolve the contradiction between X and Y.
- The study is limited in that it ignores … / it relies solely on …
- The paper fails to specify/quantify/account for …
- Smith's interpretation overlooks much of the historical research …
- However, these results were based upon data from over 30 years ago and it is unclear if …

**对比综合（contrasting sources）：**
- Unlike Smith, Jones (2013) argues that … / While Smith (2008) focuses on X, Jones (2009) is more concerned with …
- Some studies have shown the beneficial effects of X, but others have shown a deterioration in …

**总结综述：**
- Taken together, these studies support the notion that …
- Overall, these studies provide strong evidence for the efficacy of …
- However, such studies remain narrow in focus, dealing only with …

**重要研究点名式：**
- Smith (1960) was one of the first to examine …
- In a seminal study, Smith (2019) found … / In a large longitudinal study, Smith et al. (2012) investigated …

## 二、时态与语态规则（各章节该用什么时态）

**来自 Phrasebank 各节说明 + 综合规则：**

| 章节 | 主导时态 | 语态 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction（背景） | 现在时、现在完成时 | 兼用 | 现在时陈述普遍事实；"Recent years have seen…" 用现在完成时 |
| 文献综述 | 现在完成时（总体概述）+ 一般过去时（具体研究） | 主动为主 | 引用具体某年某研究用过去时（Smith (1998) found…）；作者观点若至今有效可用现在时（Smith argues that…） |
| 目的陈述 | 实证研究用过去时（was to / set out to）；thesis/paper 陈述用现在时（aims to / examines） | 兼用 | Phrasebank 特别注明两种情形的区分 |
| Methods | 一般过去时 | **被动语态为主** | "Data were collected…"；被动凸显操作本身而非操作者。序列词 Prior to / Once / Following / then |
| Results | 一般过去时 | 被动为主 | 仅报告事实（"Just the facts"），不做解释；图表指向可用现在时（Table 1 shows…） |
| Discussion | 现在时（解释、含义）+ 过去时（重述结果） | 主动为主，大量 hedging | 解释发现是 hedge 最密集之处 |
| Conclusion | 现在完成时（has shown）+ 现在时（含义） | 主动 | 回顾用完成时，意义用现在时，未来工作用 should/needs to |

**语态经验**：Methods/Results 惯用被动；Introduction/Discussion 中"研究者作为行动者"的主动句（This study examines… / We found…）已 increasingly accepted，但避免滥用 "we"。

## 三、避免抄袭与改写（paraphrase）技巧

来自 Wilson《Academic Writing》第 37 章"五种抄袭"：

1. **Verbatim plagiarism**：逐字照抄不注明来源。
2. **Mosaic plagiarism**：只换同义词、微调措辞但保留原文结构和思路，且不注明——最常见也最危险的"伪改写"。
3. **Inadequate paraphrase**：注明了来源，但没有真正把思想转化为自己的话。
4. **Uncited paraphrase**：改写彻底但忘了引用。
5. **Uncited quotation**：直接引用但缺页码/出处。

**合格改写 = 彻底转换表达 + 注明出处**。例如原文 "during his lifetime Shakespeare was virtually unknown outside his island nation" 可合格改写为 "he was not well known outside England in his own day (Grady 265)"——句法和词汇同时重组，而非同义词替换。

**实用策略**：
- 改变句子结构（主动↔被动、拆分/合并句、改变信息顺序）；
- 改变词性（investigate → an investigation of）；
- 用自己的话先合上原文写，再核对；
- 多源综合（synthesis）：把多个来源的发现放进一个自己的框架句（Taken together, these studies suggest…）；
- 短语库句式为内容中立的"公共骨架"，直接借用不构成抄袭，但**观点和证据必须归属**。

## 四、Hedging（模糊限制语）使用原则

**理论依据**（Hyland & Milton 1997 语料库研究，见 EAP 书 Unit B7）：母语写作者与二语写作者每 55 词左右出现一个认识立场标记（hedge/booster），但分布不同——**二语写作者偏好高确定性表达（will、think、claim），母语学术写作者更多使用试探性表达（would、appear、apparent、perhaps、possible）**。中国学习者常见问题是"过于笃定"而非"过于含糊"。

**使用原则**：
1. **Discussion 与解释发现处最需要 hedging**——解释因果关系时几乎必须用 may/could/might/is likely to；
2. **报告确定的结果不 hedge，解释与推论必 hedge**——"A significant difference was found"（不 hedge）vs "This difference may be due to…"（hedge）；
3. **避免绝对化与过度概括**：用 tend to、in general、often、most、with the exception of 等限定；
4. **与贡献陈述平衡**：贡献句可用"This is the first study to…"这类强断言，但含义句回落到 suggest。

**主要 hedge 资源**：
- 情态动词：may / might / could / would / should
- 概率词：possibly, perhaps, likely, probably, it is possible that …
- 距离化句型：It is thought/believed/reported that … / It has commonly been assumed that … / According to Smith (2002), …
- 源动词弱化：suggest / indicate / appear to / seem to（对比 show / demonstrate）
- 谨慎解读提示语：These results need to be interpreted with caution. / We cannot exclude the possibility that …
- 限定语：in general, generally, often, in most cases, to some extent, with one or two exceptions

## 五、使用建议

1. **先功能后句子**：写作前先确定本段要完成的功能（move），再从对应类别中选取骨架句改造；
2. **避免连续照搬**：同一类别短语变着用，避免模板痕迹（连续段落都以 "This study…" 开头是危险信号）；
3. **学科校准**：Phrasebook 区分 humanities（author-prominent：Smith (2010) argues…）与 sciences（information-prominent：X has been shown to… (Smith, 2010)），按目标期刊惯例选择引用风格；
4. **hedging 密度自检**： Discussion 中每段至少 1-2 处 hedge；Results/Methods 中几乎不需要。
